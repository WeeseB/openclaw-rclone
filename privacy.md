# Privacy policy for OpenClaw rclone

OpenClaw rclone is a personal app used only by its owner, Wissam Boustany.

It uses Google Drive access to copy files between the owner's own server and the owner's own Google Drive. It does not access anyone else's data.

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Files stay in the owner's Drive and on the owner's server.

Contact: wissam.j.boustany@gmail.com
